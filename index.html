Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded?

PDF tools process your selected documents in browser memory. There is no PDFMivo document-upload endpoint. Third-party fonts and libraries still make ordinary network requests.

Can I convert scanned PDFs?

There is no OCR in this edition. Text exports need PDFs with embedded text; scans may produce empty results. Scan to PDF combines selected image files.

Does compression always make my file smaller?

No. The tool optimises PDF structure without reducing image resolution. Results vary and the output can be larger. Keep your original.

Will Word and Excel preserve my layout?

Word exports extracted text into DOCX. Excel groups text into cells. Images, complex tables and original layouts may not be preserved. Review your result.
